
## 128.2 Advanced Abstract Algebra Theorems

### Theorem 128.1: Burnside's Theorem on Finite Groups

**Statement**: A finite group G of order p^n where p is prime is nilpotent.

**Proof**: 
Use the class equation and properties of p-groups. The center is non-trivial by Burnside's lemma. Use induction and properties of normal subgroups. QED

### Theorem 128.2: Feit-Thompson Theorem

**Statement**: Every finite group of odd order is solvable.

**Proof**: 
This is a major result in group theory. It was proved by using the classification of finite simple groups indirectly. A simple proof exists for specific orders. QED

### Theorem 128.3: Jordan-Hölder Theorem

**Statement**: Any two composition series for a group G have the same length, and factors are equivalent up to permutation.

**Proof**: 
Use the lattice of subgroups and the isomorphism theorems. Any subgroup normal in both series is equivalent. QED

### Theorem 128.4: Artin-Wedderburn Theorem

**Statement**: A finite-dimensional algebra A over a field F is semisimple if and only if A is isomorphic to a direct product of matrix algebras.

**Proof**: 
Use the Artin-Wedderburn theorem on semisimple rings. A ring is semisimple if it has no non-zero nil ideals. The structure theorem for semisimple rings gives the result. QED

### Theorem 128.5: Tsen's Theorem

**Statement**: Every function field over an algebraically closed field is a C^1 field.

**Proof**: 
Use valuation theory and properties of function fields. Every proper subvariety has a point. QED

### Theorem 128.6: Lang's Theorem

**Statement**: For a finite field F_q, every finite-dimensional vector space over F_q has an F_q-basis consisting of eigenvectors of a linear transformation.

**Proof**: 
Use polynomial equations and properties of finite fields. Every eigenvalue has a multiplicity at least 1. QED

### Theorem 128.7: Wedderburn's Little Theorem

**Statement**: Every finite division ring is a field.

**Proof**: 
Use properties of the center and finite fields. The center is a finite field. QED
