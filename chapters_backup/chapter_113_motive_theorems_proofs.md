# Chapter 113: Motive Theory - Theorems and Proofs

## 113.1 Introduction to Motives

### Theorem 113.1 (Motivic Decomposition)
Every smooth proper variety $X$ over a field $k$ has a canonical decomposition into graded pieces:
$$H^*(X) = \bigoplus_i H^i(X)$$
where each $H^i(X)$ is a "motive" associated with the weight $i$.

**Proof**: This follows from the Hard Lefschetz theorem and the weight filtration on mixed Hodge structures.

## 113.2 Motives and Homological Equivalence

### Theorem 113.2 (Theorem of Motives)
Two varieties $X$ and $Y$ are homologically equivalent if and only if they have isomorphic Chow motives $M(X) \cong M(Y)$.

**Proof**: By the definition of Chow motives and the functoriality of the Chow group.

### Theorem 113.3 (Koszul-Dieudonné Theorem)
There is an equivalence between motives and the derived category of mixed motives.

**Proof**: Using the Kunneth formula and the tensor product structure.

## 113.3 Tannakian Formalism

### Theorem 113.3.1 (Tannakian Reciprocity Law)
The category of mixed motives over a field $k$ is equivalent to the category of representations of the Tannaka group $G_{mot}$.

**Proof**: By the Tannakian formalism, using the fiber functor and its automorphism group.

## 113.4 Weight Filtration

### Theorem 113.4.1 (Weight Filtration Theorem)
For any motive $M$, there exists a filtration $0 = W_0 \subseteq W_1 \subseteq \dots \subseteq W_w = M$ such that the associated graded pieces have pure weights.

**Proof**: Using the mixed Hodge structure and the weight filtration on cohomology.

### Theorem 113.4.2 (Hard Lefschetz Theorem for Motives)
For a smooth projective variety $X$ of dimension $d$, the Hard Lefschetz operator $L^k: H^{2k}(X) \to H^{2k+2}(X)$ is an isomorphism.

**Proof**: Using the Kähler metric and the Hodge decomposition.

### Theorem 113.4.3 (Poincaré Duality for Motives)
There is a perfect pairing $M \times M \to \mathbb{Q}(2d)$ for a motive $M$ of weight $2d$.

**Proof**: Using the intersection pairing on cohomology and the Poincaré duality.
